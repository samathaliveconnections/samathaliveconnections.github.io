Recruitment Case Studies

Examples of the recruitment problems I work on and the approach I take.

01 — Niche Technology Hiring

Challenge: Limited availability of candidates with specialised technology skills.

Approach: Refine the requirement, identify adjacent skill sets, build Boolean search combinations and map relevant talent pools.

Focus: Quality of profiles, market availability and realistic candidate expectations.

02 — Banking & Financial Services Hiring

Challenge: Roles requiring both technology capability and BFSI domain knowledge.

Approach: Target relevant banking talent pools, evaluate domain exposure and validate project-level experience.

Focus: Domain relevance combined with technical capability.

03 — AI / Emerging Technology Hiring

Challenge: New technology roles where conventional keyword-based sourcing may not identify the right profiles.

Approach: Break the role into core technologies, adjacent skills, project experience and transferable capabilities.

Focus: Understanding the technology before screening the candidate.
